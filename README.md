# stock_news


# 🌍 AI Stock Market News Bot

An AI-powered Stock Market Assistant built using Python, Flask, Groq AI, and NewsAPI.

This chatbot provides:

- 📈 Stock market information
- 🌍 Latest world business news
- 📰 News headlines with images
- 🤖 AI-generated explanations
- 💹 Premium trading dashboard UI

---

# 🚀 Features

## ✅ AI Chatbot
Ask finance and stock-related questions.

Example:
- Why is Tesla stock rising?
- Latest Bitcoin news
- What happened in world economy today?

---

## ✅ Live Business News

Fetches:
- World market news
- Finance headlines
- Crypto news
- Stock market updates

Using:
- NewsAPI

---

## ✅ News Images

Shows:
- News thumbnails
- Company-related images
- Visual finance cards

---

## ✅ Elegant Trading UI

Includes:
- Glassmorphism interface
- Animated stock lines
- Market ticker
- Neon trading theme
- Smooth animations

---

# 🛠️ Technologies Used

| Technology | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Python | Backend |
| Flask | Web Framework |
